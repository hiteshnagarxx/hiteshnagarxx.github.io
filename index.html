AIF Uploader

AIF Uploader is a private, internal tool used by the owner of the Hollywood Movie Explainer and Web Series Explainer YouTube channels (English, Spanish, French and Portuguese) to upload his own recap videos to his own channels and to set their titles, descriptions, tags, captions and playlists through the YouTube Data API.

It is not offered to the public and is used only on channels the owner controls.
